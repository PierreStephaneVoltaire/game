# Food nutrition sources

## Scope and reproducible method

This note is the primary-source research ledger for the canonical catalogue’s **110 food records plus Salt Tablet (111 sourced records total)**. Non-food items and Painkillers carry explicit not-applicable provenance in the compiled catalogue.

- **Foundation foods:** exact records from USDA FoodData Central’s [April 30, 2026 Foundation JSON archive](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/fdc-datasets/FoodData_Central_foundation_food_json_2026-04-30.zip). Foundation is USDA’s analytically derived data type; see the [USDA data-type documentation](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/data-documentation/). The selected serving is the record’s FDA reference amount customarily consumed (RACC) when present, otherwise an exact published household portion.
- **Prepared foods:** exact records from USDA [FNDDS 2021–2023 JSON](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/fdc-datasets/FoodData_Central_survey_food_json_2024-10-31.zip), the as-consumed dietary-survey data type. The archive is the FNDDS component carried by the April 2026 full FDC snapshot. Each row records both FDC ID and FNDDS food code.
- **Serving arithmetic:** USDA nutrient amounts are per 100 g. Per-serving values are calculated as amount × selected gramWeight ÷ 100; kcal and sodium are shown to 0.1, grams to 0.01, and caffeine to 0.1. Foundation energy uses nutrient 2047, falling back to 2048/1008; total sugar uses 1063, falling back to 2000. FNDDS uses 1008/2000. Other IDs are protein 1003, water 1051, sodium 1093, and caffeine 1057. See USDA’s [download field descriptions](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/portal-data/external/dataDictionary).
- **Source types:** the ledger uses the validator’s exact values: `manufacturer_label`, `usda_foundation`, `usda_fndds`, `fictional_seeded_profile`, and `not_applicable`. Every USDA row spells out `FDC ID <digits>` so catalogue integration does not have to infer identifiers from URLs.
- **Branded foods:** exact first-party product pages/labels, not USDA branded approximations. A label omission is null, not zero. Doritos’ “<1 g” sugar and Dr Pepper’s approximately stated caffeine remain qualified in provenance.
- **Dates:** FF-2026-04-30 and FNDDS-2024-10-31 are source snapshot dates; R2026-08-22 is the retrieval date. USDA confirms the April 2026 releases on its [download page](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/).
- **Null policy:** — means null with the row’s explicit reason. Caffeine reported as 0 by FNDDS remains 0. Variable recipes use the named representative record; they are not claims about every preparation.

## Implementation constraints exposed by the research

1. The runtime record needs both a numeric value and an optional label qualifier. Doritos total sugar is “<1 g” and Dr Pepper caffeine is “approximately 41 mg”; a plain number-or-null field loses source fidelity.
2. Açaí Bowl remains a reachable catalogue and feeding item. Its raw nutrition fields stay null, with explicit unavailable-source reasons, until an exact primary prepared-food record is selected; do not substitute FNDDS açaí juice or a generic smoothie. Its qualitative gameplay scores are manually authored behavior, not nutrition claims.
3. The Concoction’s three profiles are explicitly fictional. Select them deterministically and never cite USDA or a manufacturer.
4. Salt Tablet is an OTC sodium-chloride product, not a conventional food label. Only sodium is directly available; other requested food-nutrient fields stay null.
5. Manufacturer pages can change. Preserve product identifier and retrieval date and re-check these five labels before a later data refresh.
